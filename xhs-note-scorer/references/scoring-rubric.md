# XHS Scoring Rubric

Score out of 100, then apply risk caps. Title and cover are the core.

## Score Weights

## Viral Route Classification

Before scoring, classify the draft:

1. `日常反差商业认知` - daily object, platform, habit, job, app, or common scene reveals a surprising commercial judgment.
   - Writing difficulty: hard.
   - Score high when the draft makes readers think "原来这个日常东西还能这么看".
   - Needs: concrete object + commercial lens + contrast.

2. `赚钱信息差` - a money/business opportunity comes from information the reader did not know.
   - Writing difficulty: easy.
   - Score high when the information is concrete and novel.
   - Risk: must stay as information gap or demand observation, not exact "how to earn" tutorial.

3. `以小博大速成` - small action/time/asset creates outsized result or fast feedback.
   - Writing difficulty: medium.
   - Common hooks: `3分钟`, `24小时`, `一个动作`, `一条笔记`, `小改动`, `傻瓜式`.
   - Score high when the promise is vivid but not fake; it should help the reader see a lever, not guarantee a result.

4. `简单认知反例` - familiar correct advice without contrast or curiosity.
   - Examples: `年入百万，先学定价`, `创业要高客单`, `要做个人IP`, `选择大于努力`, `商业模式很重要`.
   - Treat as an anti-route. Do not call it high-potential unless rewritten into one of the first three routes.

If the draft does not clearly fit route 1-3, explain the route mismatch before rewriting.

### Title: 35 points

Give high title scores when it has:

- Extreme benefit or desire: 年入百万, 改命, 红利, 傻瓜式, 暴利, 野路子, 白手起家, 100万.
- Simple operation feeling: the reader senses "I can get this", without seeing step-by-step work.
- Contrast: "不是 A，而是 B", "别人以为 X，我看到 Y", "越努力越没流量", "赚100w和赚1块钱本质一样".
- A concrete cognitive doorway: a familiar platform, tool, situation, or identity that makes the claim believable.
- Curiosity without tutorial smell.

Deduct for:

- Too generic: only "红利", "改命", or "年入百万" without a new angle.
- Too concrete/tutorial-like: 如何, 步骤, 教程, 实操版, SOP, 10个, 清单, 攻略, 手把手.
- Too internal or abstract: the user must already care about the concept before clicking.
- Weak payoff: no money, identity, transformation, or status tension.
- Course-like abstractions as the main noun: 商业模式, 底层逻辑, 认知, 方法论, 三个条件. These can be used inside the body, but titles need a more clickable doorway.
- Simple cognition title with no contrast, such as `年入百万，先学定价`; correct but not curious.

Useful title formulas:

- `{平台/工具/场景}是可以傻瓜式看懂{赚钱逻辑/商业需求/生意线索}的地方`
- `{普通人/年轻人}想{年入百万/改命}，先看懂{反常识机制}`
- `{极致结果}靠的不是{大众动作}，而是{商业判断}`
- `{身份/经历}，{极致结果}，我才明白{反常识结论}`
- `{未来几年/这个时代}最大的红利，不是{表面机会}，而是{底层能力}`
- `{日常事物/平台}不是{表面用途}，而是{商业入口}`
- `{3分钟/24小时/一个动作}看懂{高价值结果背后的机制}`

### Cover: 20 points

If no cover or cover text is supplied, score cover as 0-8 and say it is not assessable. Do not pretend to predict cover CTR.

Give high cover scores when it has:

- One dominant phrase, ideally 6-16 Chinese characters.
- One highlighted high-desire word: 年入百万, 改命, 红利, 傻瓜式, 暴利, 野路子, 白手起家.
- A visible contrast or tension, such as `别找项目 / 找需求`, `不是努力 / 是模式`, `普通人 / 年入百万`.
- Low reading burden: no dense paragraphs, no 12345 list, no tiny explanatory text.
- Alignment with the title rather than repeating a weaker version of it.
- A route signal: daily contrast, information gap, or small-to-big lever.

Deduct for:

- Too much text, too many ideas, or no visual hierarchy.
- "教程", "副业", "零成本", "项目" language.
- A cover that exposes detailed execution before the click.
- Memo/checklist covers that list the article's framework, such as `非标 / 高客单 / 个人IP`, unless the first visual layer is one dominant conflict line.

### First Sentence: 15 points

The first sentence must create contrast, immediate gain, and a reason to continue.

High-score patterns:

- `普通人以为 X，其实真正赚钱的是 Y。`
- `我以前也以为 X，直到我在 Y 里看到 Z。`
- `能不能年入百万，很多时候不是执行力，而是你第一眼能不能看懂需求。`
- `别人打开平台找工作，我打开平台看谁愿意付钱。`
- `我以前以为 X 是关键，后来才发现真正卡住普通人的其实是 Y。`

Deduct when the first sentence:

- Repeats the title without adding tension.
- Starts with background info only.
- Starts with a list or tutorial promise.
- Makes a hard money claim and immediately explains how to do it.
- Promises a familiar lesson only, such as "选择大于努力", without hinting at a new mechanism.

### Middle Body: 15 points

The body should deliver a unique insight with live-human texture and clear 获得感.

Give high scores when it:

- Uses a personal case, friend/client/team story, former job experience, or real observation.
- Explains a commercial judgment, demand insight, business model, or content/IP mechanism.
- Gives the reader a feeling of "I understand the game now" without giving a step-by-step earning manual.
- Keeps examples vivid but not operationally complete.
- Provides one new, repeatable takeaway: a mechanism, distinction, naming, or judgment the reader did not already have.
- Turns a familiar topic into a fresh lens, such as "流量大但收入低" instead of generic "要选对商业模式".

Deduct when it:

- Becomes `第一步/第二步/第三步`.
- Becomes `第一/第二/第三`, `三个条件`, `三点`, or a note-like framework too early.
- Tells the reader exactly what to sell, where to sell, and how to execute.
- Reads like a side-hustle course, project promotion, or training ad.
- Has only slogans and no concrete lived example.
- Stacks too many proof numbers. Keep one or two strong numbers, then return to the user's emotional/business insight.
- Gives only老生常谈 advice: 选择大于努力, 商业模式很重要, 要做个人IP, 要高客单价, 要差异化, 要认知升级.
- Leaves the reader thinking "我知道啊", even if every sentence is correct.

### Takeaway And Novelty Gate

Before assigning final potential, identify the note's `一句话获得感`.

Strong examples:

- `普通人不是不能做流量，而是不能做只能靠流量卖时间的生意。`
- `标准化市场看起来大，其实正是普通人最不该进去的地方。`
- `粉丝不是资产，能把信任变成高客单判断的粉丝才是资产。`
- `很多人创业失败，不是不会做内容，而是把内容当成了生意本身。`

Weak examples:

- `选择大于努力。`
- `商业模式很重要。`
- `普通人要做个人IP。`
- `创业要高客单价。`
- `要有差异化。`
- `年入百万，先学定价。`

If the only takeaway is a weak example, lower the middle-body score and be conservative with exposure potential.

### Ending: 10 points

The ending should create emotional resonance, satisfaction, and a like impulse. Treat the final sentence as the note's emotional release, not a summary. A strong ending makes the reader feel "这句话说到我心里了", "原来我不是一个人", "我终于被点醒了", or "这口气终于顺了".

High-score ending patterns:

- `普通人真正缺的不是努力，而是提前看懂需求的能力。`
- `你以为这是赚钱方法，其实这是普通人重新拿回选择权的方式。`
- `年入百万不是突然开挂，是终于不再用打工脑袋看世界。`
- `爽点要落在认知被点透，不要落在马上去照做。`
- `很多人不是输给了能力，而是输给了从来没人教他怎么看见钱流向哪里。`
- `普通人最爽的那一刻，不是突然赚到钱，而是终于看懂自己以前为什么一直被困住。`
- `真正的改命感，是你第一次发现，原来世界一直在给你留入口，只是你以前看不见。`

Deduct when it:

- Ends with generic encouragement.
- Ends with a direct CTA to learn, join, buy, or follow a tutorial.
- Repeats the same sentence-level claim without emotional release.
- Ends by summarizing the framework, method, or steps instead of creating 共鸣.
- Feels correct but cold: it teaches the reader what to do, but does not make them feel seen, relieved, or爽.

### Risk And Safety: 5 points

Start from 5, then deduct for risk.

Hard forbidden or severe risk:

- `零成本`
- `副业培训`
- `赚钱教学`
- `创业项目推广`
- Explicit income proof plus exact operational instructions.
- "照着做", "保姆级", "手把手教你赚钱", or numbered steps attached to money-making.

Soft risk:

- `教你赚钱`, `实操版`, `SOP`, `教程`, `项目`, `玩法`, `复制`.
- Lists of money-making projects.
- Claims like "普通人快速赚到100万" without framing as认知 or商业判断.
- Course-like certainty: `成功率自动提升`, `满足这些条件就超越95%`, `我教大家一个简单方法`.

Safer replacements:

- `教你赚钱` -> `看懂赚钱逻辑`, `看懂商业需求`, `看懂生意线索`.
- `项目` -> `机会`, `需求`, `生意线索`, `商业入口`.
- `步骤/教程` -> `判断框架`, `底层逻辑`, `商业认知`.
- `零成本` -> `低试错`, `轻资产`, `先验证`.
- `三个条件` -> `三个信号`, `我后来只看这几个信号`.
- `商业模式` in the title -> `生意选错了`, `第一天就注定亏`, `赚不到钱的模式`.

## Risk Caps

Apply these caps after scoring:

- Contains hard forbidden terms: max 59.
- Looks like side-hustle training or money tutorial: max 69.
- Explicit money result plus concrete execution steps: max 72.
- No title or no body: max 60.
- No cover supplied: max 82, unless the user asks to score copy only.
- Strong title but weak/risky body: max 78.
- Title is concept-heavy and cover leaks the framework: max 72.
- Body uses dense income proof plus numbered entrepreneurial conditions: max 69.
- Body has no new takeaway beyond common business advice: max 68.
- First sentence is strong but the body delivers only老生常谈: max 72.
- Draft is simple cognition anti-route with no contrast/curiosity: max 62.
- Draft claims fast results like `3分钟` or `24小时` with concrete earning guarantees: max 69.
- Money information-gap draft becomes step-by-step赚钱教程: max 69.
- Actual result has near-median read rate but very low exposure: diagnose as first-pool progression failure, not merely low CTR.

## Post-Publication Diagnosis

When the user supplies actual exposure and reads, calculate:

`read rate = reads / exposure`

Compare it to the June baseline cover CTR/read-rate proxy:

- Below 9%: likely title/cover click failure.
- 9%-16%: click is not the main problem. If exposure is below 1,500, likely first-pool progression failure.
- Above 16% with low exposure: click was decent; inspect engagement, saves, comments, risk signals, topic pool, and account distribution.

Use this diagnosis order:

1. Exposure and reads: did it get an initial pool?
2. Read rate: did title/cover earn clicks?
3. Engagement and saves if supplied: did readers signal value?
4. Takeaway value: did readers get a new idea, or only familiar advice?
5. Viral route: did it use daily contrast, money information gap, or small-to-big lever?
6. Content structure: did it become a lesson, checklist, or money tutorial?
7. Topic/title doorway: is the main noun concrete enough for 小红书?

For `300 exposure / 40 reads`, read rate is 13.3%, close to baseline. The most likely failure is not pure cover failure, but "首轮后没有继续推". Diagnose likely causes: abstract title doorway, memo-cover answer leakage, tutorial-like body structure, dense income proof, or risk-sensitive money/entrepreneurship framing.

## Exposure Potential Bands

Use the June 2026 baseline:

- `低潜`: below 1,500 likely, or below median unless fixed.
- `普通`: 1,500-3,000 style potential.
- `可发`: 3,000-6,000 style potential, likely above median.
- `高潜`: 6,000-50,000 style potential, has top-quartile signals.
- `爆款候选`: 50,000+ potential, only when it resembles the strongest pattern: familiar platform or scenario, "傻瓜式" simple insight, high money desire, strong cover, live case, and low tutorial risk.

Never present these as guaranteed impressions. Say "按你 6 月数据，更像..." instead of promising numbers.

For concept-heavy entrepreneurship posts, be more conservative: do not call them `可发` unless the title has a concrete doorway and the cover does not leak the framework.

For posts without a fresh takeaway, do not call them `高潜` even if the title is clickable. High exposure needs both click desire and post-click value.

For simple cognition anti-route posts, default to `低潜` or `普通` until rewritten into one of the three viable routes.
